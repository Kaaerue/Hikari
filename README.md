
# 🌸 Hikari — Personal Life Companion

Hikari is a personal life companion app designed to help you organize your daily life, build better habits, and manage your personal finances.

Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, Hikari focuses on simplicity, personalization, and offline-first functionality.

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 **Home Dashboard** — Overview of your daily activities.
- 🌱 **Habit Tracker** — Track and manage your daily habits.
- 💰 **Money Tracker** — Record income and expenses.
- 📝 **Notes** — Save your personal notes.
- 🎨 **Appearance Settings** — Light, Dark, and System themes.
- 💖 **Personalization** — Customize your name and greeting.
- 📱 **Offline-first** — Your data is stored locally on your device.

## 🛠️ Built With

- Kotlin
- Jetpack Compose
- Material 3
- Android DataStore
- SharedPreferences

## 📱 Requirements

- Android 7.0 (API 24) or later.
- Internet connection is not required for basic functionality.
